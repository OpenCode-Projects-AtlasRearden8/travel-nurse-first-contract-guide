# Appendix B — The Packing Checklist

*Copy this list into your own notes app and delete what doesn't apply to your assignment (warm city vs. cold city, provided housing vs. stipend). The philosophy is in Chapter 6: documents on your person, first-night box in the cabin, everything else light.*

## Documents (on your person, always)

- [ ] Driver's license / state ID
- [ ] Nursing license (physical card + photo/PDF copy)
- [ ] Multistate license documentation (NLC paperwork if applicable)
- [ ] CPR/BLS card
- [ ] Immunization records + TB results
- [ ] Health insurance card + medication list
- [ ] Emergency contacts (written, not just in phone)
- [ ] Offer letter / assignment confirmation
- [ ] Agency contact info (24-hour number)
- [ ] Direct deposit info (voided check or bank letter)
- [ ] Any required certifications/training records

## First-night box (in the car cabin, never buried)

- [ ] Sheets + pillow (+ pillowcase)
- [ ] One towel + washcloth
- [ ] Toiletries bag (double-check contacts/glasses + solution)
- [ ] Phone chargers (car + wall)
- [ ] Medications + a few OTC basics (pain reliever, antacid, bandages)
- [ ] Change of clothes for Day 1
- [ ] Snacks + water bottle
- [ ] Instant coffee/tea + one mug
- [ ] Phone + phone + ID + wallet (obviously, but it's the trip)

## Clothing

- [ ] 2–3 weeks of socks + underwear
- [ ] Scrubs for week one (even if facility provides; bring a set for credentialing/fitting)
- [ ] Comfortable shoes — and one broken-in backup pair
- [ ] Compression socks if you wear them
- [ ] One professional outfit (for hospital orientation day)
- [ ] Weather layer for the region: warm coat or sun shirt
- [ ] Sleep/wallow: one loungewear set that isn't scrubs

## Work bag

- [ ] Badge reel + lanyard
- [ ] Watch with second hand (or phone habit — check your unit's rules)
- [ ] Stethoscope (they don't usually supply these)
- [ ] Pen, small notebook
- [ ] Snack bars for the shift that runs
- [ ] Water bottle that fits the unit's holder
- [ ] Food containers for packed shifts

## Kitchen

- [ ] Check with your housing: what counts as furnished varies wildly
- [ ] One good knife + cutting board (the furnished ones are usually dull)
- [ ] Spices/sauces that make you feel human (small, checked or carried)
- [ ] Coffee setup — your one luxury

## Bathroom/self-care

- [ ] 2-week supply of routine items, reorder locally
- [ ] Hair dryer if your hair has opinions
- [ ] First-aid basics for your own weekend accident

## Mood / comfort items (small, intentional)

- [ ] One photo frame or printed photo
- [ ] Charger + cables for tablet/book reader
- [ ] Streaming passwords (log into the TV app before you leave)
- [ ] Your candle/blanket. You're allowed.

## Before you lock the front door (home checklist)

- [ ] Mail forward / hold
- [ ] Bills on autopay (verify each one)
- [ ] Someone local: name + number who has your spare key
- [ ] Car maintenance if driving (oil, tires, brakes, wiper fluid)
- [ ] Home-state records filed where you can find them (Chapter 7 folder)
- [ ] Home-state voter/mail/vehicle registrations: confirm they live where you claim to live (matters for licensing — Chapter 3)
