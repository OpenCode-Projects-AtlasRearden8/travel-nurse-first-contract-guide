# Appendix A — Negotiation Questions

*Bring this list to recruiter calls and interviews. Education only — asking these questions is routine and professional; answering them is the agency's job.*

## About the job itself

1. What unit will I float to, and what competencies must I have to float there?
2. What is the patient-to-nurse ratio on a typical shift?
3. What scheduling system do you use, and how far out are schedules posted?
4. Am I guaranteed the shift type (days/nights) I'm accepting?
5. What does orientation look like — length, and who provides it?
6. Is there an on-call requirement, and what is the on-call rate?

## About guaranteed hours and cancellations

7. What are my guaranteed hours per week, and is that in the written contract?
8. If the *hospital* cancels my shift, do guarantee hours still pay?
9. If *I* call off, how is the guarantee affected — that shift, or the whole week?
10. What is the hospital's policy on low-census cancels, and who absorbs the loss?
11. What notice does the agency owe me if it cancels the contract, and vice versa?
12. If the assignment ends early, what compensation applies to unused days — housing, travel, completion bonus?

## About pay

13. What is the taxable hourly rate, and how are overtime hours calculated?
14. What stipends are included (housing, meals & incidentals) — per week, and for how many weeks?
15. Are stipends paid during scheduled PTO weeks?
16. What are the holiday rates, and are holiday shifts required by contract?
17. Is there a completion bonus, and is it prorated for approved extensions?
18. What reimbursements exist (licensing, travel, parking, scrubs, certifications), and what are the caps?
19. When is payday, and how are corrections handled?

## About housing

20. Is housing provided or stipend-based — and if provided, is it a private unit or a shared one?
21. What happens if I need to break the housing arrangement for a hospital-side cancellation?
22. Who do I call when something breaks at 10 p.m.?
23. What arrives furnished — bed, linens, kitchenware — and what should I bring?

## About licensing and compliance

24. Which license does this assignment require — multistate, or a specific state license?
25. What is your compliance timeline, and who is my compliance point of contact?
26. Are licensing fees reimbursed for assignments that require a new state license?

## About the agency

27. Who is the employer of record — your company or a subcontracted agency?
28. Is this position W2 with taxes withheld? *(Seek a tax professional for anything beyond this.)*
29. Is this job inside an MSP/VMS consortium? Who holds the hospital contract?
30. Can you send me the written contract before I accept, so I can review it before making a decision?
31. What is your after-hours contact process for on-shift problems?

## About extending

32. Is there an extension option, and by what date must both sides decide?
33. Do extension contracts carry the same rates, or can they change?
34. What happens to my housing if I extend but the unit lease ends?

## How to use this list

- **Ask, then write.** Jot the answer next to each question or as fast as your thumbs can type afterward.
- **Anything verbally promised but not in writing is a "hope."** Ask when it will be in writing. That's normal.
- Re-run the pay-and-cancellation questions for **every new contract**, even from an agency you trust and love. Terms drift between clients.
